services

I contribute to the materials science community through conference leadership, editorial activities, peer review, technical committees, and academic service. My activities have increasingly focused on developing technical communities around materials processing, defect engineering, magnesium alloys, and data-driven materials design.

🎤 Conference Leadership & Organization

  • Lead Organizer, AI-Enabled Materials Processing: Integrating Accelerated Experimental Workflows and Processing-Aware Machine Learning, TMS Annual Meeting 2027
    Leading symposium development with collaborators from academia and national laboratories to establish a cross-disciplinary forum connecting experimental materials processing with AI and machine learning.

  • Co-Organizer & Session Chair, Vacancy Engineering in Metals & Alloys, TMS Annual Meeting 2026
    Co-developed the symposium and helped build cross-committee support connecting vacancy engineering with processing, phase transformations, structural materials, and ICME.
    Session Chair: Vacancy Engineering in Various Applications

  • Session Chair, Magnesium Technology 2026: Biomedical Applications, Corrosion Mechanisms and Characterization, TMS Annual Meeting 2026

  • Discussion Leader, Gordon Research Seminar on Physical Metallurgy 2025
    Invited to moderate the Processing–Structure Relationships session.

  • Organizer, Slip, Twins & Voids Symposium, MACH Conference 2023
    Invited to co-organize a session on advanced deformation mechanisms.
