🇨🇭 **Starting My Postdoctoral Research at ETH Zürich**

I am delighted to share that I have started as a **Postdoctoral Researcher** in the **[Laboratory of Metal Physics and Technology (LMPT)](https://metphys.mat.ethz.ch/)** at **ETH Zürich**, working with **[Prof. Jörg F. Löffler](https://mat.ethz.ch/department/people/person-detail.loeffler.html)**.

---

### Looking Ahead

At ETH Zürich, I am building on my background in **physical metallurgy, non-equilibrium processing, microstructure–property relationships, and advanced materials characterization** while exploring new research directions.

My research will focus on understanding how **atomic-scale defects and local structure evolve in crystalline alloys and metallic glasses**, particularly under irradiation and other non-equilibrium conditions. I am interested in connecting these changes to material properties and exploring how **vacancies and free volume can be engineered** to improve performance.

I also look forward to developing new experimental capabilities, learning from colleagues, and pursuing collaborative research across materials science.
