🎤 **Invited Seminar at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory**

I delivered an invited technical seminar at **Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL)** titled:

**“Hidden States That Govern Performance in Materials for Extreme Environments.”**

---

### Talk Summary

The seminar brought together several themes from my doctoral research around a common question: **how can hidden microstructural and defect states be identified, controlled, and ultimately used to design better materials?**

I discussed how processing can generate non-equilibrium defect populations and microstructures that strongly influence subsequent material behavior, even when those states are difficult to capture using conventional descriptors.

Examples included my work on **vacancy-induced solute clustering, thermomechanical processing of magnesium alloys, biodegradable metallic materials, and data-driven materials design**.

The broader theme was the need to connect **atomic-scale mechanisms, processing history, microstructural evolution, and engineering performance** when designing materials for demanding environments.
