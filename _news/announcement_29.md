🎉 **Editorial Board Appointment**

Sreenivas Raguraman has been elected to the **Editorial Board of *JOM – The Journal of The Minerals, Metals & Materials Society (TMS)***, as part of the **Magnesium Committee**, following his nomination and selection at TMS 2025.

---

### 🧪 About *JOM*  
*JOM* is a flagship journal published by Springer and TMS, covering innovative research in minerals, metals, and materials science. With a long-standing reputation in the community, the journal serves as a platform for advances in structural materials, alloy development, processing, and performance.

As a board member, Sreenivas contributes to the journal’s scientific direction through:
- Manuscript review and editorial guidance  
- Curating high-quality magnesium alloy research  
- Supporting the TMS community’s publishing goals

---

### 🧭 Why This Matters  
This appointment recognizes Sreenivas’ growing leadership in the field of **magnesium alloy development**, **biodegradable metals**, and **advanced processing science**. It also reflects his commitment to advancing peer review and shaping future materials research.

> “It’s an honor to serve the materials community through *JOM* and contribute to its legacy in disseminating impactful research.”
