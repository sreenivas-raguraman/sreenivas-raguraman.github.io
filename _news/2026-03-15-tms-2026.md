🎤 **TMS 2026 Annual Meeting, San Diego**

I participated in the **TMS 2026 Annual Meeting** in San Diego through research presentations, symposium organization, and session leadership across several areas of physical metallurgy and magnesium research.

---

### Research Presentations

I presented two oral talks:

**“How Alloying Elements Shape Vacancy-Induced Solute Clustering in Binary Magnesium Alloys”**

This talk focused on how alloy chemistry influences vacancy-solute interactions and the development of solute clustering following non-equilibrium processing.

**“When Processing Leads the Way: ML-Powered Optimization of Biodegradable Mg Alloys”**

This presentation discussed our use of experimental feedback and machine-learning-guided exploration to navigate processing pathways for biodegradable magnesium alloys.

---

### Symposium & Session Leadership

I also served as a **Co-Organizer** of the symposium:

**Vacancy Engineering in Metals & Alloys**

The symposium brought together researchers working across vacancy physics, phase transformations, structural materials, processing, and computational materials science.

I additionally served as a **Session Chair** for:

- *Vacancy Engineering in Various Applications*
- *Magnesium Technology: Biomedical Applications, Corrosion Mechanisms and Characterization*

TMS 2026 was an especially rewarding meeting because it brought together several parts of my research, from **atomic-scale defect engineering to processing, machine learning, and biodegradable magnesium alloys**, while also providing the opportunity to help build technical communities around emerging research areas.
