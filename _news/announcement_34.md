🧠 **Comment Article Published in *Nature Reviews Materials***

Excited to share our new Comment, **“A call to elevate the role of processing in AI-driven materials design,”** co-authored by **Sreenivas Raguraman**, **Adam Griebel**, **Maitreyee Sharma Priyadharshini**, **Paulette Clancy**, and **Timothy P. Weihs**. 

---

### What the Piece Argues  
AI is accelerating materials discovery — but **processing** still decides whether predicted materials can actually be made and perform as intended. We advocate making processing a **first-class design axis** in models, datasets, and workflows, not an afterthought. 

> “The future of materials design lies not just in what we might make, but also in **how** we make it.”
