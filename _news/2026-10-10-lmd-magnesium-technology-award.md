🏆 **LMD Magnesium Technology Student Paper Award**

I am honored to be a **co-recipient of the LMD Magnesium Technology Award – Student Paper** from **The Minerals, Metals & Materials Society (TMS)** for our work presented at the **TMS 2026 Annual Meeting & Exhibition**.

---

### Award-Winning Work

**Paper:** *“Disentangling the Effects of Cyclic Mechanical Loading and Corrosion on the Degradation of Braided Magnesium Alloy Stents”*

The work examines the combined effects of **cyclic mechanical loading and corrosion** on the degradation of braided magnesium alloy stents, an important consideration in the development of biodegradable metallic implants.

---

I am grateful to my collaborators and co-authors for their contributions and pleased to see our work recognized by the magnesium research community.
